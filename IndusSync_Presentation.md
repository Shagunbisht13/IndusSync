# IndusSync
## AI-Powered Industrial Product Intelligence

**Transform fragmented product inputs into structured, commerce-ready information.**

---

## The Problem
- Messy, fragmented supplier data.
- Hard to enforce validation rules and schema structures.
- Time-consuming manual extraction of product attributes.

---

## The Solution: IndusSync
- **Data Parsing:** Handles messy supplier strings automatically.
- **AI Enrichment:** Powered by Gemini 2.5 Flash to extract specifications and dimensions.
- **Strict Compliance:** Enforces character limits and casing rules for commerce descriptions.
- **Evaluation Engine:** Built-in validation checks against ground truth guidelines.

---

## Technical Stack
- **Frontend:** Streamlit for rapid interactive prototyping and visualization.
- **Backend Pipeline:** Python-based extraction and evaluation scripts.
- **AI Model:** Google Gemini GenAI SDK.
- **Data:** Structured CSV inputs and outputs matching delivery formats.
